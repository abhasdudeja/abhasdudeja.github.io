About Me

Moving Cities Through
Data-Driven Planning

I'm a transport planner working on how cities move people — and how their bus fleets go electric. I'm currently Senior Transport Planner at the Centre of Excellence in Urban Transport (CoEUT – CRDF), CEPT University, Ahmedabad, where I'm planning the deployment of 1,000+ electric buses across eight Gujarat cities under the PM e-Bus Sewa scheme and advising the State of Chhattisgarh on its roll-out.

Before this I led the Transportation vertical at Microgrid Labs for close to four years, delivering fleet transition plans and charge-management deployments for transit operators in India, the USA, Italy and the Philippines — including the 900-bus electrification strategy for Bengaluru's BMTC. I'm an Associate Member of the Institute of Town Planners, India, and a firm believer in public transport and accessible, sustainable mobility for all.
